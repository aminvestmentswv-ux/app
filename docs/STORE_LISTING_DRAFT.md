Explore AM Investments properties in West Virginia. Browse Available, Coming
Soon, Under Contract and Sold collections, search by address or city, and view
published property details and photographs.

See bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage and descriptions when the official

## Reviewer notes draft

No sign-in or payment is required. On the home screen, search an address/city and
switch among Available, Coming Soon, Under Contract and Sold. Open a property to
view published specifications, description, photos, original source and sharing.
Native maps open externally and use the listing's address, not device location.
The company phone button appears only for Coming Soon details.

Installed apps check the validated company inventory feed at launch and on return
to foreground, with a manual Refresh properties action. If unavailable or stale,
the last valid in-memory snapshot or the bundled snapshot remains visible with
its checked time. No real-time availability claim is made. Native push is not part
of this release. Shared HTTPS property links open the existing web application;
