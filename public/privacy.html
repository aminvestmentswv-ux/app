AM Investments app privacy policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Who operates the app

A&M Properties and Investments LLC, trading as AM Investments, operates the AM Investments property app. This policy covers the installed iOS and Android app. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to aminvestmentswv@gmail.com or to 132 Harris Dr, Poca, WV 25159-9648, United States.

Browsing and information on your device

You can browse without creating an account. Address and city searches, selected filters and the current inventory snapshot are handled in app memory. The app does not send search text to a search or analytics service. The current version does not offer registration, payments, native push subscriptions or advertising. It does not request precise device location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Images and other public resources may be cached by the operating system.

Network requests and service providers

The app requests public inventory from app.aminvestmentswv.com, hosted on GitHub Pages, and property photographs from image-cdn.carrot.com. These connections disclose your IP address and technical request information to the service handling the request. GitHub states that Pages visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security. Hosting and image-delivery providers may process request information to deliver content, maintain reliability and protect their services. Their own privacy statements describe their practices. AM Investments does not operate a separate app visitor analytics, advertising or lead-tracking system, and does not sell app user data or use app browsing for targeted advertising.

Links, maps, sharing and contacting us

When you choose a map, original listing, support page or shared link, the app may open another app or website. Maps use the published property address, rather than your device location. A sharing action supplies the property link to the destination you select; you decide whether to send it. Those destinations follow their own privacy practices. The company website and web version may have browser features, cookies and notification choices that are separate from the installed app. If you contact us by email or phone, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond to your request.

Retention, security and your choices

We use HTTPS for inventory and photo downloads. No method of transmission or storage provides an absolute security guarantee. Hosting and image-provider records follow those providers' retention practices; we do not promise a fixed deletion period for their logs. We retain information you send directly to us only as needed to handle the request, maintain necessary business records or meet legal obligations. You may email the privacy contact to ask about access, correction or deletion of information we hold about you. Requests are subject to identity verification when needed and applicable legal requirements. Closing the app clears its in-memory browsing state; you can clear app storage or uninstall to remove app-managed local data. Uninstalling does not delete provider security logs or correspondence already sent.

Children and policy changes

The property app is intended for a general audience interested in real estate and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We may update this policy as the app or our practices change; the published policy will show its updated effective date.
